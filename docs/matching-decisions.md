# Matching decisions: one engine, chosen on the corpus

Plan step 5 of `plans/phase-3-lineup.md` (goal reset: best-of by a labelled corpus, labels automatic). Each of the 58 rows of `docs/matching-rules-inventory.md` (plus orchestrator-settled Appendix C rows) is one rule where the four matchers disagree; this table picks the unified engine's variant per row and records the evidence. Step 6 implements `data/corpus/decisions.json` (row id -> variant). Generated by `scripts/corpus/decide.py`; do not edit.

## Method

- **Labels.** `data/corpus/pairs.jsonl` (sizes in `data/corpus/STATS.json`). Construction truth: a synthetic provider spelling of canonical X is X when it keeps the feed, and a different channel when it does not (`X West`/`X Pacific`/`X (W)` and `X +1` are not `X`; `X East` is, the US convention SM and LU both encode); near-miss canonicals, real channels some normalizer folds together, other stations of the same network or market, and an alias of a near-miss are `different`; undisputed aliases of the merged table (human-curated) are `same` in both directions. Real pairs no generator labels get oracle labels: unanimous, else majority of four (ties `unknown`). The four oracles are not independent (JAN, CM and LU share the core; CM's override is sourced from LU), so majority labels are never used to decide a row by ablation; they only feed the whole-path rows, which are weak at best.
- **Generators are neutral.** Every synthetic form of a family (quality tags, prefixes, zones, `+1`, decoration, invisible characters) is included only if real names carry it (`STATS.json` `generator_forms`: counts over the merged DBs, lineups, aliases and every plugin test file) and then drawn uniformly, so each plugin's distinctive table entry is tested exactly as often as the shared ones. Strong rows need most of their deciding pairs on forms seen at least 5 times for real.
- **Attribution by ablation.** Whole-path oracles differ in many rules at once, so a split between two plugins is not evidence for one rule. `scripts/corpus/ablate.py` builds one base engine (Lineuparr's `match_all_streams` over the shared core, in LU's isolated namespace), swaps ONE rule at a time to each plugin's own table, statement or function, and scores only the pairs where the swap changes the verdict, on construction labels only (unanimous and majority labels include the base's own vote). Interacting rows are searched jointly (zone words with the zone filter; prefix table, prefix order and geographic table; digit guards with designator repair; the whole callsign ladder; alias lookup with the alias-callsign rescue) and the configuration is settled by coordinate descent, so the decisions are one engine.
- **Cost** = 2*FP + FN on the discriminating pairs (a wrong attach/assign/rename is worse than a miss). Ties and rows with fewer than 10 discriminating pairs take the strictest variant (unless those few pairs favour another by more than noise: then `weak`); costs within 2 of the cheapest count as a tie. **strong**: >= 20 discriminating pairs, winner >= 20% and >= 3 cheaper than the runner-up, the same winner at base cuts 80 and 90, and evidenced forms; **weak** otherwise; **no-evidence** below 10 pairs, for inactive mechanisms, and for rows the plan fixes by design; **orchestrator** for conflicts the corpus cannot settle (the plugins' own tests disagree, Appendix C), decided by the orchestrating agent and recorded in `decide.py` `ORCHESTRATED` with the reason.
- **Rows no ablation isolates** (whole decision paths: C06, D04-D06, D10, T02-T05, T08, T12, Z03, Z05) keep the whole-oracle comparison on substring-hinted pairs and are capped at `weak`.

Decided 58 rows: **6 strong / 20 weak / 31 no-evidence / 1 orchestrator** over 28450 pairs (20950 construction). Coordinate descent converged in 3 rounds.

- holds: Designator repair (N08) exactly when the digit guard (T07) reads designator tokens.
